# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase; inferred from the explicit implementation brief

## Users

People using a mobile-first gratitude journal. Assumed primary focus is personal reflection with public sharing as a second use case.

## Product Purpose

Let people write, save, read, and share gratitude entries with low friction.

## Positioning

A gratitude journal that combines private journaling, public feed discovery, guest use, and account-backed persistence in one flow.

## Operating Context

Mobile web first. Users write short entries, choose visibility, browse a feed, edit profiles, and report content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Gratitude entries can be public, anonymous, or account-linked.
- Guests can create entries before signup.
- Guest entries migrate to an account after authentication.
- Supabase is the backend foundation.
- MVP must support moderation, profile pages, and responsive navigation.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: BeGrateful.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the user brief and repository scaffold. No existing content, assets, or claims to preserve beyond the requested product scope.

## Product Principles

- Keep writing fast.
- Treat privacy as a first-class choice.
- Make public sharing optional, not forced.
- Preserve guest work through signup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-friendly, keyboard-safe, and usable without requiring a logged-in account.
